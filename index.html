About UCC

UAE CAR CLUBS

مجموعة أندية السيارات في الامارات 🇦🇪

Established in April 2019, UAE CAR CLUBS (UCC) brings together car and bike enthusiasts under one powerful community. Our mission is to promote the passion for automotive culture across the UAE through exciting motor events, social gatherings, and unforgettable experiences.

We focus on building a positive, friendly, and diverse environment where enthusiasts can connect, share their passion, and grow together.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the UAE's most influential and innovative automotive community, uniting car enthusiasts of all backgrounds while setting the benchmark for world-class motor events across the region.

Our Mission

To create exceptional automotive experiences by organizing high-quality, safe, and engaging motor events that bring together a diverse community of enthusiasts — fostering passion, connection, and growth within the UAE's automotive culture.

What Drives Us

Core Values

Passion for Automotive

We live and breathe cars, driven by a deep passion to celebrate and grow the automotive lifestyle in the UAE.

Community & Inclusivity

With members from over 30 nationalities, we embrace diversity and create a welcoming environment for all enthusiasts.

Excellence in Execution

With 185+ successful events, we deliver high-quality, well-organized, and unforgettable automotive experiences.

Safety First

We prioritize the safety and well-being of our members, participants, and audiences in every event we produce.

Innovation & Growth

We continuously evolve, bringing fresh ideas and new experiences to stay ahead in the UAE automotive scene.

Integrity & Professionalism

We operate with transparency, reliability, and respect in all our partnerships and engagements.
