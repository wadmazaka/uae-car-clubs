About UCC

UAE CAR CLUBS - UCC

🇦🇪 مجموعة أندية السيارات في الامارات

Established in April 2019, UAE CAR CLUBS (UCC) brings together car and bike enthusiasts under one powerful community. Our mission is to promote the passion for automotive culture across the UAE through exciting motor events, social gatherings, and unforgettable experiences.

We focus on building a positive, friendly, and diverse environment where enthusiasts can connect, share their passion, and grow together.

Our Vision & Mission

Driving the future of automotive culture in the UAE

Our Vision

To be the UAE's most influential and innovative automotive community, uniting car enthusiasts of all backgrounds while setting the benchmark for world-class motor events and experiences across the region.

Our Mission

To create exceptional automotive experiences by organizing high-quality, safe, and engaging motor events that bring together a diverse community of car enthusiasts. We aim to foster passion, connection, and growth within the UAE's automotive culture while continuously elevating standards through creativity, professionalism, and inclusivity.
